Youth Resources & Assistance

IRS Free Tax Filing

Eligible Direct Filing Tools

  • IRS Direct File: Eligible taxpayers can file their federal tax returns online directly with the government for free through a secure, interview-style system.
  • IRS Free File: Taxpayers under specific income thresholds can access free, brand-name commercial tax preparation software to file their federal returns.

Localized State Filing Integration

  • NY State File: New York provides a dedicated, direct online portal allowing residents to submit their state income tax returns at zero cost.
  • Integrated Software: Most approved software options automatically link federal data directly to the New York state system to simplify filing.

NY Legal Aid & Services

Youth Employment & Rights Advocacy

  • Legal Aid Society: High school students can access specialized legal guidance regarding workplace discrimination, wage theft, and minor labor law violations.
  • Youth Justice Clinics: Local organizations offer direct consultations to help young workers navigate unfair scheduling and independent contractor misclassification.

Educational & Academic Resources

  • PALS Program: Practicing Attorneys for Law Students provides mentoring, legal literacy workshops, and educational guidance tailored for young students exploring the legal field.
  • Public Library Law Guides: The New York Public Library (NYPL) system hosts free legal reference materials, citizenship workshops, and legal aid clinics across the boroughs.

Financial Literacy Guides

Foundational Personal Finance

  • Budgeting Infrastructure: Free educational guides teach students how to track income, manage checking accounts, and understand automatic payroll deductions.
  • Credit Education: Resource modules outline how credit scores operate, the risks of high-interest debt, and how to safely build credit as a young adult.

Wealth & Goal Planning

  • Savings Frameworks: Interactive toolkits guide youth on establishing emergency funds and using compound interest to achieve long-term financial stability.
  • Tax Literacy Basics: Specialized youth financial packets explain the real-world math behind tracking gross pay versus estimated take-home earnings.
