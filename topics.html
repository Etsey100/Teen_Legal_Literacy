W-2 vs. 1099 Breakdown

Tax Handling

  • W-2 Employees: Employers execute automatic tax withholdings from each paycheck and directly cover half of the employee's FICA contribution.
  • 1099 Contractors: Contractors receive gross compensation without deductions, assuming full responsibility for a 15.3% self-employment tax.

Operational Control

  • W-2 Employees: Management determines fixed work hours, designated locations, and specific protocols for executing daily professional responsibilities.
  • 1099 Contractors: Independent business entities retain complete autonomy over their operational schedules, production methods, and client portfolios.

Benefits & Protections

  • W-2 Employees: Personnel receive statutory workplace protections alongside employer-subsidized benefits like health insurance and retirement matching.
  • 1099 Contractors: Individuals must independently finance their own benefits but can leverage business expense deductions to lower taxable income.

NY Minor Labor Laws

Working Papers

  • Ages 14 and 15: Enrolled students must secure blue employment certificates through their school, which require annual physician verification.
  • Ages 16 and 17: High school students must upgrade their documentation to green employment certificates to remain legally employable.

Hour Limits

  • Ages 14 and 15: Work schedules are restricted to a maximum of 3 hours on school days and 18 hours total per week.
  • Ages 16 and 17: Academic-year employment limits expand to 4 hours on school days and a maximum of 28 hours weekly.

Safety Rules

  • Permitted Work: Minor labor is encouraged in secure, professional environments such as retail sales, office support, and software development.
  • Banned Work: The state strictly prohibits minors from high-risk environments, including active construction sites and heavy factory manufacturing floors.

Tax Fundamentals Explained

Tax Brackets

  • Progressive Structure: Income is segmented into distinct tiers, meaning your entire earnings pool is never taxed at a single rate.
  • Marginal Application: Advancing into an elevated bracket applies the higher tax percentage exclusively to the dollars within that specific threshold.

Deductions vs. Credits

  • Tax Deductions: Adjustments lower your overall financial liability indirectly by decreasing the total volume of income subject to taxation.
  • Tax Credits: Incentives offer a more substantial financial benefit by applying a direct, dollar-for-dollar reduction to your final tax bill.

FICA Taxes

  • Social Security: A flat 6.2% payroll deduction is automatically allocated to fund federal retirement, disability, and survivor infrastructure.
  • Medicare: A flat 1.45% payroll deduction is routinely collected to support healthcare infrastructure for senior citizens.
